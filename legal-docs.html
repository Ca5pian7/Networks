Part 1

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data Syntra Bot ("the Bot", "we") collects when it operates in a Discord server, why, and how it's handled. This applies to every server the Bot is added to, referred to here as "partner servers."

What we collect

  • Discord user IDs — of message authors, reporters, and reported users. We use IDs rather than usernames wherever possible, since usernames change.
  • Message content — only when a message is reported (via the report command or the report reaction), filtered by a word filter, or posted in a connected global-chat channel for relay to other partner servers.
  • Report evidence — text, links, or descriptions submitted by moderators when filing a report.
  • Server-level configuration — channel IDs you register (alerts, global chat, ads, mod-log), your server's registered network level, and word filter word lists you set.
  • Case and moderation history — records of reports, tier assignments, broadcasts, appeals, and their outcomes.

We do not collect passwords, payment information, private DMs (other than appeal conversations you initiate with the Bot), or any data beyond what's needed for the features above.

How we use it

  • To operate safety reporting: filing, reviewing, and broadcasting alerts about reported users across partner servers.
  • To operate global chat relay between servers that opt in.
  • To operate word filtering as configured by each server's own admins.
  • To operate partnership ad delivery, targeted by server size ("level"), which is derived only from member count.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a local database operated by the network administrator and is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or third parties outside the partner server network. Case data (reports, tiers, broadcasts) is visible only to the hub server's Review Team and Network Admins. Server-level configuration is visible only to that server's own admins.

Data sharing across the network

By design, when a report is reviewed and confirmed at Tier 2 or Tier 3, the reported user's Discord ID and the report category are shared with every partner server's alert channel. This is the core function of the network and is disclosed here so server owners and members understand what participation involves.

Your rights & data removal

If you'd like data about you removed or corrected (for example, an overturned report), contact the Network Admin at the address below. Overturned cases are retracted network-wide using the same broadcast mechanism as the original alert.

Children's privacy

The Bot is intended for use in accordance with Discord's own Terms of Service, which require users to meet Discord's minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect data from users who do not meet that requirement.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Bot's features change. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date.

Part 2

Terms of Service

By adding Syntra Bot to a Discord server, or by using its features (including reporting, global chat, or word filtering) as a member of a partner server, you agree to these terms, in addition to Discord's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

What the Bot does

Syntra Bot connects a network of partner Discord servers for two purposes: cross-server safety reporting (identifying and alerting partner servers about users who violate community safety standards) and optional global chat relay between servers.

Acceptable use

  • Reports must be filed in good faith with supporting evidence. Filing knowingly false reports to harass or target another user is a violation of these terms and may result in the reporting server's removal from the network.
  • Server owners are responsible for the word filter lists they configure locally.
  • Server owners are responsible for how they act on network alerts within their own server — the network provides information, not enforcement.

The tier system

Reports are reviewed by a Review Team and assigned a tier (1: logged only, 2: network alert, 3: severe/escalated). Tier 3 cases are also recommended for direct reporting to Discord Trust & Safety and, where relevant, law enforcement — the network does not replace those channels.

Appeals

Any user subject to a network alert may appeal. Appeals are reviewed by the Review Team and may result in a retraction broadcast to all partner servers.

Removal from the network

A partner server may be removed from the network at the discretion of Network Admins for misuse of the reporting system, repeated bad-faith reports, or violation of these terms. A server may also leave voluntarily at any time by removing the Bot.

Disclaimer

The Bot and network are provided as-is. We make reasonable efforts to ensure accurate moderation decisions but cannot guarantee the accuracy of every report or the conduct of any user. The network is a tool to assist server moderation, not a substitute for each server's own judgment and rules.

Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated as the Bot evolves. Continued use of the Bot after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
