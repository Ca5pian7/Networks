Partner Agreement

Syntra Network — Partner Charter

Version [1.1]  •  Effective [1/9/2026]

01Purpose

This Charter sets out what it means for a Discord server to join the Syntra Bot partner network, a group of independent Discord communities that share safety information to identify and contain harmful users, and optionally connect for cross-server chat.

By registering your server with the network (running the /setup commands), your server's owner or admin agrees to this Charter on behalf of the server.

02What membership means

  • Your server keeps full control over its own rules, moderation, and members, the network does not override your server's autonomy.
  • Your server appoints one Network Liaison — the point of contact for network matters.
  • Your server can file reports on users who violate safety standards, using evidence-backed reports via /report.
  • Your server will receive network-wide alerts about confirmed Tier 2/3 cases, and decides locally how to act on them (ban, kick, watch, or ignore).
  • Your server may connect to optional features: global chat relay, partnership ads, participation in these is optional even after joining.

03Reporting standards

Reports must include real evidence such as message links, screenshots, or a specific factual account. Vague accusations without evidence will be rejected by the Review Team.

Reports are reviewed before any network-wide action. Filing knowingly false or malicious reports is a violation of this Charter and may result in your server's removal from the network.

04Tier system & your responsibilities

  • Tier 1 — logged only, no action needed from your server.
  • Tier 2 — network alert sent to your Liaison's alert channel. Your server decides what to do.
  • Tier 3 — severe cases. In addition to the network alert, we recommend your server also report directly to Discord Trust & Safety, and to law enforcement or NCMEC where relevant (e.g. child safety threats). The network does not replace these channels.

05Data sharing you're agreeing to

When a report from any partner server is confirmed at Tier 2/3, the reported user's Discord ID and report category are broadcast to every partner server's alert channel including yours, and including cases reported by other servers, not just your own. This is the core mechanism of the network.

Full details are in the network's Privacy Policy.

06Appeals

Any user flagged by a network alert can appeal via /appeal from any partner server. Appeals are reviewed by the Review Team; if upheld, a retraction is broadcast network-wide the same way the original alert was.

07Removal from the network

A server may be removed from the network for: repeated bad-faith reports, misuse of global chat, failure to appoint an active Liaison, or violation of this Charter. A server may also leave voluntarily at any time by removing the Bot.

08Acknowledgement

By running /setup alerts_channel, /setup global_channel, or /setup liaison in your server, an authorized admin of your server confirms they have read and agree to this Charter and the network's Privacy Policy & Terms of Service on behalf of the server.
